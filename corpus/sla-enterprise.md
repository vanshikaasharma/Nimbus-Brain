# Enterprise SLA

This policy applies only to customers on the Enterprise plan.

## Availability

We target 99.9% monthly uptime for the ingest API.

## Credits after an ingest outage

If a named incident lasts **60 minutes or longer** and an Enterprise account is on the impacted list, that account is owed a service credit.

Credit amount: **10% of that month’s invoice**, applied to the next bill.

The credit is not automatic for Starter or Pro. It is not owed if the account was not listed on the incident.

Example: if Acme is Enterprise and was listed on an ingest outage that ran longer than an hour, the SLA says we owe Acme 10% of that month’s invoice.
