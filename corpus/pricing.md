# Nimbus pricing

Nimbus is a usage-based ingest API. Customers pay a monthly platform fee plus usage (per million events).

## Starter

- Platform fee: $99 / month
- Included usage: 2 million events
- Rate limit: 50 requests / second

## Pro

- Platform fee: $499 / month
- Included usage: 20 million events
- Rate limit: 200 requests / second
- Overage: $12 per extra million events

## Enterprise

- Platform fee: $2,000 / month
- Included usage: 100 million events
- Rate limit: 1,000 requests / second
- Overage: $8 per extra million events
- Covered by the Enterprise SLA (see sla-enterprise.md)

Accounts on Enterprise right now include Acme and Soylent.
